What is Summit Path?

AI is already part of how people work, study, and make things. Understanding it should not depend on being able to pay for a course.

Some of the best teaching on the subject is already free. It is spread across university sites, company academies, and video channels, and it is hard to know where to start.

Summit Path puts it in one place, sorted by what you already know, so anyone who wants to learn can pick a course and begin.

We assign levels from what each course says you need to know, such as coding experience or math. They are our judgment, not the provider's label.

Each course stays on its provider's own site. Summit Path does not host, copy, or resell any of it.

Free can have limits

Some courses charge for certificates or ask for a free account. The Cost line on each card says which.

Course details can change. Confirm cost and requirements on the provider's site before you start.
